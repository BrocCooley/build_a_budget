Local resources

Food help in and around Fargo-Moorhead, Minneapolis, and NDSU. Hours and eligibility change, so check each site or call before you go. Links checked November 2026.

Fargo-Moorhead

Great Plains Food Bank: partner food pantries

The region's food bank. Its Get Help pages list partner pantries and meal sites.

Emergency Food Pantry

Food pantry at 1101 4th Ave. N., Fargo.

Dorothy Day Food Pantry (Churches United)

Food pantry in Moorhead. The page lists current hours and needed items.

FirstLink 2-1-1

Dial 2-1-1 for referrals to food and other help in North Dakota and Minnesota, 24/7.

Minneapolis

Minnesota Food HelpLine (The Food Group)

Call 1-888-711-1151 for help finding food shelves and applying for SNAP.

Second Harvest Heartland: find food

Food resource map for food shelves near you, plus help applying for SNAP.

City of Minneapolis: food resources

The city's list and map of food shelves and meal programs.

NDSU

Goods for the Herd Food Pantry

Campus food pantry for NDSU students. Appointments are required after completing the annual intake form.

NDSU Little Free Pantry

Two take-what-you-need pantries on campus (Memorial Union and Barry Hall). No application needed.

NDSU Dean of Students: food resources

NDSU's overview of campus food help.

NDSU Dining: food and financial resources

Dining Services' list of meal and money help for students.
